Profile

Building intelligence into future networks

I am a PhD researcher in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the University of Glasgow, United Kingdom, where I started on 1 October 2026. My research direction is AI-native digital twins, intelligent network automation, and trustworthy AI-driven optimisation for 6G-and-beyond wireless communication systems.

I hold a master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Inha University, South Korea. My previous research spans reinforcement learning for 5G-NR-V2X resource management, RSU-assisted message fusion for autonomous driving, LEO satellite communications, RIS-assisted systems, and integrated sensing and communication.
